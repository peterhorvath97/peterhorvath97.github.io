Chapter 1 — Monetary Transmission in a Fragmented Uncertain World

CONFERENCE-READY · WITH TOHID ATASHBAR

Monetary Transmission in a Fragmented Uncertain World

How economic uncertainty affects monetary policy transmission. Using Bayesian threshold VARs across 30 economies, the paper finds that inflation responds markedly less to monetary shocks in high-uncertainty states while the GDP response remains comparatively stable.

Chapter 2 — Housing-market imbalances and monetary transmission

PUBLISHED · 2026

Nonlinear Transmission of Monetary Policy and Housing Market Imbalances: Evidence From a Factor-Augmented Threshold VAR Analysis

Housing-market conditions affect both monetary-policy behaviour and the transmission of monetary shocks. Evidence from the United States points to more gradual policy easing around housing booms and somewhat stronger contractions in activity and financial aggregates.

Journal ↗

Chapter 3 — Forward guidance and the yield curve

CONFERENCE-READY

A Distributional Perspective on Forward Guidance and the US Term Structure

Monetary policy announcements affect conditional volatility as well as the conditional mean of the US yield curve. Standard policy-rate shocks tend to increase volatility, while forward guidance compresses it, however, the volatility effects are smaller and less persistent than the mean effects.
